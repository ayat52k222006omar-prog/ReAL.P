Cybersecurity and Data Privacy

🔒 Cybersecurity Measures

At ReAL.P, we are committed to protecting our data infrastructure by using strong encryption protocols (such as HTTPS and SSL), securing databases with restricted access permissions, and regularly updating security systems to prevent any potential vulnerabilities.

🛡️ User Privacy

We are committed to protecting the confidentiality of our customers' and users' data and not sharing it with any third party, while providing users with full control over their data to ensure a secure and reliable digital environment.
