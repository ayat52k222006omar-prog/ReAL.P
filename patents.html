    • Nature of the Innovation

    The invention involves developing an advanced adsorbent material based on recycling spent aluminum cans, converting them into an active core component, and engineering them with a sustainable organic framework to form a dual-treatment platform.

    Mechanism

    The technology operates as a comprehensive, multi-functional "One-Pot" solution capable of the simultaneous and efficient removal of dual pollutants in industrial wastewater, including persistent organic pollutants (such as dyes and textiles) and toxic heavy metals.

    Technical Advantages & Novelty

    • Sustainability & Circular Economy (Waste-to-Resource): Complete reliance on recycling local aluminum waste (spent cans) and raw materials derived from natural biomass.
    • High Treatment Efficiency:
      • Removal of colored organic pollutants with a maximum adsorption capacity of up to 189 mg/g.
      • Removal of toxic heavy metals with a maximum adsorption capacity of up to 343 mg/g.
    • Multi-Pathway Operation: The technology relies on the integration of advanced physical and chemical mechanisms to ensure high-efficiency pollutant retention.
    • Superior Reusability: The material maintains over 90% of its operational efficiency after repeated regeneration cycles for more than 5 consecutive cycles using standard, economical recycling solutions.
    • Scientific Validation: Design efficiency and performance results have been documented through advanced computational modeling, with the results officially published in an international, top-tier Q1 scientific journal (Scientific Reports).

    Legal Scope & Intellectual Property Rights (IP)

    Legal Framework for Protection:

    Subject to the mechanisms of technology protection and patent registration under Egyptian Intellectual Property Rights Law No. 82 of 2002, and Law No. 23 of 2018 on Science, Technology, and Innovation Incentives.

    Type of Protection Required:

    • Protection of the green synthesis and "One-Pot" extraction method of the material from aluminum waste.
    • Protection of the innovative industrial application of the technology in wastewater and industrial wastewater treatment (for textile, battery, and electroplating factories).
